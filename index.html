What we do

Four kinds of support, one mission

The Foundation provides support to persons with disabilities and vulnerable individuals, removing barriers and addressing exclusion within society.

Medical support

Care and outreach that bring healthcare closer to those who need it most.

Emotional support

Compassion, dignity and a community that stands with every individual.

Assistive support

Assistive devices that restore mobility, independence and inclusion.

Educational support

Educational assistance so that every person has access to opportunity.

About us

Championing welfare, rights, dignity and inclusion

We Feel Your Pain Global Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to championing the welfare, rights, dignity and inclusion of persons with disabilities and vulnerable individuals.

Through compassion, partnerships, advocacy and community engagement, the Foundation works towards creating a society where everyone, regardless of disability or vulnerability, has access to healthcare, education and opportunities.
