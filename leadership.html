Leadership & Governance

Our National Secretariat

We Feel Your Pain Global Foundation operates through a centrally coordinated National Secretariat responsible for the organization's administration, leadership coordination, programs and institutional chapters across Ghana.

While the Foundation is working towards establishing its permanent national headquarters, its administrative operations are currently coordinated through its designated administrative base.

Our commitment to serving vulnerable individuals and communities remains our priority, irrespective of our physical office location.
